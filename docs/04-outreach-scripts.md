# 04 — Outreach Scripts and Offer

Merge fields are GHL's (`{{contact.*}}`, `{{custom_values.*}}`). Keep emails plain
text, short, one link max.

## Offer
- **{{custom_values.offer_name}}** — {{custom_values.offer_setup_price}} setup +
  {{custom_values.offer_monthly_price}} (hosting, edits, missed-call text-back,
  review requests, all running in their own GHL sub-account)
- Risk reversal: "You see the finished site before you pay anything."

## Dated-site #1
**Subject:** quick note on {{contact.company_name}}'s site
> Hi — I had a look at your website and noticed a few things that are likely costing
> you calls: {{contact.website_issues}}.
> Most of your customers search on their phone, so these matter. Want me to send a
> free redesign mockup?

## Breakup
**Subject:** closing the loop
> I'll stop reaching out — if a new site is ever useful, the mockup offer stands.

## Cold-call opener (for the call tasks)
> "Hi, is this the owner of {{company}}? I'm {{name}}, a local web designer. I
> noticed you don't have a website — I actually mocked one up for you. Can I text or
> email you the link?" → asking permission gives you consent to text.

## Objections
- *"I get all my work from referrals."* → Referrals Google you before calling; the site
  is what makes them call you instead of the next name.
- *"Too expensive."* → One extra job a month covers it. Offer the monthly-only plan.
- *"I have Facebook."* → Keep it; the site gets you into Google search, Facebook doesn't.
